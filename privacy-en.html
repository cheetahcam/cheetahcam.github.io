CheetahCam Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026 · Operator: CheetahCam Team · Contact: dhoguider@gmail.com

CheetahCam is built so that video, audio and images are never stored on the operator's servers.

1. Data that stays on your devices only

  • Live video/audio: sent only over an end-to-end encrypted direct connection (WebRTC, DTLS-SRTP) between your paired devices.
  • Motion recordings and thumbnails (only if you turn this on): stored encrypted on the camera device for up to 3 days (7 days with CheetahCam Plus).
  • Activity log ("Today's diary"): per-minute motion counts, stored encrypted on the camera device for 14 days (30 days with CheetahCam Plus).
  • Notification contents (who, what happened, battery, etc.) and alert settings: end-to-end encrypted on the camera device and readable only on the receiving family device.
  • Clips and photos you choose to save (CheetahCam Plus): saved to the viewing device's gallery (CheetahCam album); after that, storage, deletion and backup follow that device and your settings. This never goes through the operator's servers.
  • Paired device list, device names and security keys: stored encrypted on each device. Keys cannot leave the device.

3. Processors and international transfer

Google LLC provides Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Functions in the Seoul region, asia-northeast3; Realtime Database for camera online status in Singapore, asia-southeast1), Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob and App Check. Some data may be processed in the United States.

4. Your choices

Delete recordings, unpair devices or reset the camera at any time in the app. Uninstalling removes all on-device data.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age in your country).

6. Responsibilities of camera owners

Tell people in the monitored space that a camera is operating. Listening to other people's private conversations without consent may be illegal. Sound is off by default and recordings contain no audio.
